## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Mobile-friendly dashboard for monitoring live, VOD, and catch-up connections, stream statistics, and connected clients.
- Manual live source switching without interrupting connected clients.
- Controls to disconnect clients, stop live channels, and stop individual VOD or catch-up viewers.
- Catch-up programme details and playback progress.
- Configurable dashboard availability, port, and mount path. The dashboard defaults to port 9294 and `/switch`.
- Dispatcharr credential-based dashboard login. Requires Dispatcharr v0.34.0 or a compatible development build.
